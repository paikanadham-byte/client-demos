About Pawfect Paws

The groomer nervous dogs ask for.

Pawfect Paws is a one-to-one grooming studio on Madehurst Road in Heeley, Sheffield — built around a simple idea: dogs groom better when there's nobody else in the room.

Meet the groomer

Claire, and the calm studio.

Every dog at Pawfect Paws is groomed by Claire — the same familiar face, start to finish, every visit. Owners mention her patience again and again: with nervous dogs, skittish rescues and pets who've struggled at busier salons, she works slowly, reads the dog in front of her, and never rushes a finish.

The studio specialises in the coats that need the most care, too — doodles with their mat-prone curls and spaniels with their feathering — so the dogs other salons find "difficult" are exactly the dogs this one was set up for.

One-to-one, always

Your dog is the only dog in the studio. No cages, no waiting around other dogs, no hand-offs between groomers.

Calm before beautiful

A stressed dog can't be groomed safely or well. Breaks, sniff-arounds and slow handling come first — the smart finish follows.

Honest advice

If a coat needs clipping short for the dog's comfort, we'll say so and explain why — and show you how to keep on top of it at home.
