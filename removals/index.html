The Work

Four stops to moved in

One crew leader owns your move from survey to the last box — you always know who to call.

Survey

A 20-minute home visit or video call. We count, measure and note the awkward stuff — pianos, narrow stairs, the lot.

Pack

We pack the day before if you've booked it, label every box by room, and wrap furniture for the van.

Move

Moving day: floor and door protection down first, van loaded in room order, keys handed over when you're ready.

Settle

Boxes in the right rooms, beds rebuilt, and we take our loan boxes and packaging away on the next round.
