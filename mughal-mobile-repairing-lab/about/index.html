THE LAB

Mughal Mobile Repairing Lab is a professional phone-repair lab in the basement of Hub Commercial Plaza, Bahria Town Phase 8 — a proper bench setup with a rework station, ultrasonic cleaner and testing equipment, not a counter in a market.

The lab takes on the full range: all mobile repairing, IC repairing and repolishing, hardware and software faults — with original parts and quality work. Their promise is on the sign: your mobile, our responsibility.
