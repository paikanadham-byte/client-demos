Questions

Before you book.

Is it really one dog at a time?

Yes. Every appointment is one-to-one — your dog is the only dog in the studio, with Claire from start to finish. That's what makes it work for nervous and reactive dogs.

My dog is anxious. Can you still groom them?

Anxious dogs are a speciality here. Book an anxious dog session: we allow extra time, take breaks whenever your dog needs one, and never force a finish. If a full groom is too much in one visit, we'll build up to it over a few shorter ones.

How much will it cost?

It depends on your dog's size, breed and the condition of their coat, so we don't publish a one-size price list. Call or WhatsApp with your dog's breed and we'll confirm the exact price before you book — it won't change on the day unless the coat needs significantly more work, and we'd always agree that with you first.

How long does a groom take?

It varies with the dog and the service — a bath and tidy is quicker than a full doodle groom. Because appointments are one-to-one and unhurried, we'll give you an honest estimate when you book and text you when your dog is ready.

My doodle's coat is matted. What happens?

Tell us when you book and we'll plan for it. Where we can, we'll brush mats out gently; where a mat is tight to the skin, clipping it out is kinder — Claire will always talk it through with you before doing anything.

How do I book?

Call or WhatsApp 07555 377428, or send the short form on our contact page. Tell us your dog's breed, age and anything about their temperament, and we'll find a calm slot.

Not sure what your dog needs?

Message us a photo of your dog's coat and we'll happily advise — no obligation to book.
