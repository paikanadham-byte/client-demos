About Leo’s · Memorial Dr SE, Atlanta

The wash that still uses hands

Leo’s Hand Car Wash & Auto Detailing, at 1566 Memorial Dr SE — where the name is the method.

Plenty of places will run your car through a tunnel of brushes. Leo’s does the opposite: every car is washed by hand, the slow way, because that’s the way that gets a car actually clean without putting its paintwork through a machine.

That means the work takes as long as the car in front of us needs. When a family Outback rolled in carrying a dog’s worth of hair and a gravel road’s worth of dust, the team spent a full hour on it — inside and out — and sent it home impeccable. That review is on this page, word for word, because it says what we’re about better than we could.

You’ll find us on Memorial Drive in Atlanta, open seven days a week. Roll in dirty; roll out shining.
