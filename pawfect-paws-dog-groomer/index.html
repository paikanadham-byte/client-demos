What we do

Grooming that goes at your dog's pace.

Every appointment is one-to-one, so there's never a room full of barking strangers — just a calm studio and a groomer your dog gets to know.

Full Groom

Bath, gentle dry, cut and style, nails and ears — the complete reset, finished with a check-over.

Bath, Brush & Tidy

The in-between freshen-up: bath, brush-out, face, feet and hygiene trim to keep the coat manageable.

Doodle Coat Care

Cockapoos, cavapoos and every doodle in between — thorough brush-outs and cuts that keep curls mat-free.

Spaniel Styling

Feathering tidied, ears and skirt shaped — spaniels leave looking like spaniels, not sheep.

Anxious Dog Sessions

Extra time, extra breaks and a patient hand for nervous, rescue and skittish dogs. No sedation, no forcing it.

Puppy's First Groom

Short, gentle introductions to the sounds, smells and handling of the studio — all praise, no pressure.

The calm way

Four steps, one dog at a time.

1 · Settle in

A sniff around the studio and a proper hello. You tell us about quirks, sore spots and favourite treats.

2 · Bath & dry

Warm water and mild shampoo matched to the coat, then a gentle hand-dry — never cage-dried, never rushed.

3 · Cut & style

Clipped or scissored to the style you chose, with breaks whenever your dog asks for one.

4 · Nails, ears & home

Finishing touches and a coat, skin and ear check — with anything worth knowing passed on to you.
