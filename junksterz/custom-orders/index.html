Straight answers

How do I start a custom order?

Call or text (513) 289-5257, or send the order-request form with your idea. Tell Junksterz the garment, the team or the colors you have in mind — that conversation is step one.

Can you rework my own jacket?

That’s the core of the brand. Authentic workwear and brand pieces are exactly what Junksterz rebuilds — send yours and it comes back as a one-of-one set.

Can you do my team or my colors?

The tagline is the answer: exclusive rework of your favorite colors, materials or brands. Bengals and Chiefs pieces are in the lineup — yours can be next.

How much does a commission cost?

Every rebuild is different — it depends on the garment and the build. Price is agreed with you on the call before any cutting starts. Posted one-off pieces have gone for $120–$250, which gives you the neighborhood.

Will there be another one like mine?

No. Your piece is cut from one garment, once. That’s not a marketing line — it’s arithmetic.
