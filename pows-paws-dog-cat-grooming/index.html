What we do

Big dogs, small dogs, and the cat.

Full Groom — Dogs

Bath, dry, cut and style, nails and ears — with two groomers sharing the work so no dog is rushed or held awkwardly.

Bath & Brush

The freshen-up between full grooms: bath, blow-dry, brush-out and tidy of face, feet and hygiene areas.

Cat Grooming

Yes, really. Gentle grooming for cats — brush-outs, mat removal, hygiene trims and lion cuts where the coat needs a reset.

Emmi-Pet Teeth Cleaning

We're Emmi-Pet trained: silent, vibration-free ultrasonic teeth cleaning — no brushing motion, no stress, fresher breath.

Giant Breeds

Newfoundlands, Bernese, big doodles — all breeds and sizes genuinely welcome, with two groomers to do the heavy lifting kindly.

Nails & Finishing Touches

Nail trims, paw tidy and ear cleans as a quick visit or added onto any groom.

How it works

Finished when they're ready.

1

Settle & play

A proper hello and a sniff around first. Nervous pets get as long as they need before anything starts.

2

Two-groomer groom

One groomer comforts and holds, the other grooms. Calmer for the pet, safer for wiggly bits, kinder all round.

3

Bath, dry & style

Warm bath, gentle dry and the cut or tidy you asked for — checked by four eyes before we call it done.

4

Home time

Ready when they're ready — not when a slot timer says so. That's the whole point of Pows Paws.
