Our Work

The dogs we do best.

Three kinds of appointment walk through our door more than any others. Each one is one-to-one, unhurried, and finished only when the dog in front of us says so.

Doodle Coats

The doodle transformation

The problem: a thick, curly doodle coat that's started to felt behind the ears and under the collar — the classic doodle trouble spots.

What we do: a full brush-out before the bath (never wash a mat — it only tightens it), a curl-friendly dry, then a teddy trim shaped to the dog.

The result: a soft, mat-free coat and a simple brushing routine for home, so it stays that way.

Spaniels

The spaniel reset

The problem: feathering full of Peak District mud, ears tangled, and a skirt that's stopped looking like a spaniel at all.

What we do: bath and condition, feathering thinned and shaped, ears and feet tidied — the classic outline, kept practical.

The result: a spaniel that looks like the breed again, and dries in half the time after wet walks.

Illustrative example

Anxious Dogs

The nervous dog breakthrough

The problem: a rescue dog who shakes at the sound of clippers and has never managed a full groom anywhere.

What we do: start with a short visit — sniff around, treats, the dryer on low across the room. Build up over sessions, at the dog's pace, with breaks whenever they're asked for.

The result: a dog who can be groomed calmly and safely — and an owner who stops dreading groom day.
