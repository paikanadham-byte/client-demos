Why rework?

Anybody can print a logo on a blank hoodie. Junksterz starts further back — with the actual garment. A real work jacket. A real team hoodie. Denim that’s already been somewhere. Then it gets rebuilt into women’s sets and game-day outfits that carry all of that history in a shape nobody’s seen before.

“Exclusive rework of your favorite colors, materials or brands.”

That’s the brand’s own line, and it’s the whole method. Your colors. Your materials. Your brands. A Bengals dress with Burrow 9 for Cincinnati. A Chiefs dress with Mahomes 15 for Kansas City. A Carhartt set built from canvas that was made to be worked in, not posed in.

And because every build starts from a single existing garment, every piece is one of one by construction — not a limited run, not a marketing drop. When it’s claimed, there is no warehouse with more. There’s just the next garment waiting to be cut.

Junksterz is a clothing brand run as Junksterz SHOP LLC out of Cincinnati, Ohio, selling the way its customers already talk to it: directly, by phone and text, piece by piece.
