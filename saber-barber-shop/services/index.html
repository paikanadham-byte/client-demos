Good Questions

Before you sit down

Do I need to book, or can I just walk in?

Both work. Walk-ins are welcome every day we're open — if the chairs are full there may be a short wait. Booking ahead guarantees your slot: call or WhatsApp 07413 564518, send the form on our contact page, or find us on Fresha.

When are you open?

Monday to Saturday, 9:30–18:00, and Sunday 9:30–17:00 — seven days a week, on Roundhay Road in Harehills.

How much is a haircut at Saber Barber Shop?

Prices are kept affordable — it's the word regulars use most in our Google reviews — and the full price list is on the board in the shop, confirmed before anything comes off. Call or WhatsApp us and we'll tell you straight.

Where exactly are you?

160–162 Roundhay Road, Harehills, Leeds LS8 5PL — on the main Roundhay Road strip. The directions link on our contact page drops a pin right on the door.

It's my first visit — what should I ask for?

Bring a photo of what you like if you have one, and tell the barber how you style it day to day. We'll be honest about what suits your hair and how much upkeep it needs before anything comes off.

Do you cut children's hair?

Yes — kids cuts are a regular part of the day here: patient, quick, and parents are welcome right next to the chair.
