Questions

Before you book.

Why two groomers on every dog?

One groomer supports and comforts your dog while the other grooms. It's calmer for the pet, safer around scissors and clippers, and much kinder for big, elderly or nervous dogs who shouldn't have to stand unsupported.

Do you really groom cats?

Yes — cats are a proper part of Pows Paws, not an afterthought. Brush-outs, mat removal, hygiene trims and lion cuts, all done gently and at the cat's pace.

How long will the groom take?

As long as your pet needs. Our rule is “groom to the dog, not the clock” — we don't rush pets through fixed slots. We'll give you a rough guide when you book and message you when your pet is ready to collect.

What is Emmi-Pet teeth cleaning?

Emmi-Pet is an ultrasonic toothbrush we're trained to use. It cleans without sound, vibration or brushing movement, which makes it ideal for dogs who hate having their teeth done. It helps with plaque and bad breath as part of regular sessions.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your pet's breed, size and coat condition, so rather than publish a misleading price list, we confirm your exact price when you book — call or WhatsApp with your pet's breed and we'll quote before you commit to anything.

My pet is nervous. Is that a problem?

Not here. Unhurried appointments and a second pair of hands are exactly what nervous pets need. Tell us when you book and we'll plan a gentler session, with breaks whenever your pet asks for one.

Tell us about your pet.

Dog or cat, tiny or giant — message us the breed and we'll quote and book you in.
