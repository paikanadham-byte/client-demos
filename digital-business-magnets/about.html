About us

The agency behind the attraction

Digital Business Magnets is an eCommerce agency based on Bahria Expressway in Bahria Town Phase 4, Islamabad. We exist for one kind of client: the owner of a product brand — clothing, cosmetics, consumer goods — who has something worth buying and needs the online presence to match.

Plenty of brands start the way our clients did: great products sold through Instagram, messages and word of mouth, with nothing pulling new customers in. Our job is to build the magnet — a professional store, a brand image customers trust, content that gets noticed, and a growth plan that says what happens next — and then to support the brand while it grows.

We are open 24 hours, every day, because online stores do not keep office hours and neither do the questions that come with them.

How we work

  • Direction first. Clear direction and practical planning before anything is designed.
  • Everything connected. Store, branding, social and creatives built to pull together.
  • Support that stays. Consistent support throughout the process — not just at launch.
  • Honest proof. Our reputation is our Google reviews, shown in full below.
