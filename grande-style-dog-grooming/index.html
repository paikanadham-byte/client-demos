The Groom

Four paws, four steps

Every appointment follows the same gentle path, at your dog's pace — follow the trail down the side of this page and you'll get the idea.

1

Check-in & sniff-around

Ten minutes to explore the salon, meet their groomer and settle. You tell us about quirks, sore spots and favourite treats.

2

Bath, with the gentle stuff

Warm water, mild shampoo matched to their coat, and a proper massage. Double-coated dogs get the full de-shed treatment here.

3

Dry, cut & style

Fluff-dried by hand (never cage-dried), then clipped or scissored to the style you chose — breed standard or something more fun.

4

Finishing touches & report card

Nails, ears, paw balm and a bandana. Your report card notes coat condition, skin, ears and anything worth watching.
