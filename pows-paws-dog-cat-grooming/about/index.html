About Pows Paws

Two groomers. Zero hurry.

Pows Paws is a dog and cat grooming studio in Sheffield S5, run on one rule that explains everything else we do.

“We groom to the dog, not the clock.”

That's our slogan, and it's a working rule, not a poster quote. Pets don't read timetables — a nervous dog needs ten minutes of hello before the bath, a giant breed needs breaks, a cat decides for itself when it's had enough. So we don't book pets into fixed slots and rush them through. We book the pet, and the groom takes as long as it takes.

Why two groomers?

Four hands are kinder.

Every dog at Pows Paws is groomed by two people. One supports, comforts and reassures; the other bathes, clips and styles. It means no dog is left standing unsupported, no wiggly leg is held at a bad angle, and big breeds — we take all breeds and sizes — are groomed safely instead of wrestled.

We're also Emmi-Pet trained, offering silent ultrasonic teeth cleaning for dogs who'd never accept a normal toothbrush.

Dogs and cats

Most groomers pick one. We groom both — properly, gently, and at each animal's own pace.

All breeds, all sizes

Teacup to giant, smooth to double-coated. Two groomers means nobody gets turned away for being too big.
