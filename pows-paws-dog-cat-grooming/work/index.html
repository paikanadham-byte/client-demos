Our Work

The grooms we're known for.

Three appointments sum up Pows Paws better than any gallery: the giant, the cat, and the smile. All groomed the same way — to the pet, not the clock.

Giant Breeds

The two-groomer giant groom

The problem: a 50kg double-coated giant is a wrestling match for a lone groomer — exhausting for the dog and risky around clippers.

What we do: one groomer supports and reassures, the other bathes, dries and brushes. The dog leans into a person, not a restraint.

The result: a fully groomed giant who stays calm throughout — and owners who stop being turned away elsewhere.

Cats

The cat, groomed properly

The problem: a long-haired cat with mats forming under the legs and tummy — uncomfortable, and getting worse every week.

What we do: a quiet session at the cat's pace: mats gently worked out or clipped where they're tight to the skin, a hygiene trim, and breaks whenever the cat calls time.

The result: a comfortable cat with a manageable coat — no sedation, no drama.

Illustrative example

Emmi-Pet

The silent teeth clean

The problem: plaque building up and breath to match — in a dog who panics at the sight of a toothbrush.

What we do: Emmi-Pet ultrasonic cleaning. The brush is silent and still — no vibration, no scrubbing — so the dog barely notices it happening.

The result: cleaner teeth and fresher breath, built up gently over regular sessions.
