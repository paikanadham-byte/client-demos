About Northgate

Same chairs.
Same standards.

Northgate Barbers opened its doors at 214 Oldham Road in 2016, and the deal we made with the street then is the deal we still keep now: a proper cut, done properly, for a fair price — with the price on the board before you sit down, not invented at the till.

We're an old-school shop in the ways that matter. Hot lather on every neck shave. A cut-throat razor for the shaves. Time taken over a consultation instead of a guess with the clippers. And modern in the ways that help: book from your phone in under a minute, fixed prices on everything, and an average fade turned around in about 25 minutes when you're on your lunch.

"No upsells, no surprises. The price you see is the price you pay — that's the whole business model."

Most of our chairs are filled by regulars and the people they send us. Kids get the same patience as anyone — under 12s are £12, with a parent right next to the chair. Whoever you are, you get whoever's chair is free, and every chair works to the same standard: if it isn't right, it isn't finished.

You'll find us two minutes from Piccadilly Gardens, on the 83, 84 and 181 bus routes, with street parking on Oldham Road. Walk in any day we're open — or book ahead and skip the wait.
