Good Questions

Before you sit down

Do I need to book, or can I just walk in?

Both work. Walk-ins are always welcome Monday to Saturday, 9:00–19:00 — if the chairs are full there may be a short wait. Booking ahead guarantees your slot and skips the queue.

How long does a skin fade take?

About 30 minutes, including the razor-finished edges, the hot-lather neck shave and styling. If you're on a lunch break, tell us — we'll keep it sharp and on time.

How much is a haircut at Northgate Barbers?

A classic cut is £16 and a skin fade is £18. The full list is above — the price you see is the price you pay, with no extras added at the till.

Do you take card payments?

Yes — card and cash are both fine.

It's my first visit — what should I ask for?

Bring a photo of what you like if you have one, and tell the barber how you style it day to day. We'll be honest about what suits your hair and how much upkeep it needs before anything comes off.

Do you cut children's hair?

Yes — kids cuts for under 12s are £12, take about 20 minutes, and parents are welcome right next to the chair.

Pick a service. We'll keep the chair warm.
